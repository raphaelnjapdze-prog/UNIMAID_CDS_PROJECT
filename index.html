Vector surveillance, field to policy

Turning mosquito data into malaria decisions.

VDAM KC — short for Vector Dynamics and Malaria Knowledge for Control — is a surveillance and analytics platform for vector-borne disease control — built for the realities of endemic regions: patchy connectivity, multiple vector species, and rising insecticide resistance. We help teams see risk before it becomes an outbreak.

How it works

From a trap in the field to a decision at the ministry

Three steps, running continuously, not as a one-off report.

01 — Collect

Field data, on or offline

Field teams log trap counts, larval habitat surveys, and insecticide-resistance bioassays from a phone or tablet, with or without a signal.

02 — Analyze

Raw counts become risk

VDAM KC turns species-level counts and resistance results into district-level risk scores and hotspot maps, updated as new data arrives.

03 — Act

Resources follow the model

National malaria programmes and partners direct nets, spraying, and larviciding to the areas the data shows are rising — before case counts spike.

The platform

One dashboard, from trap data to policy

VDAM KC brings entomological field data, resistance testing, and case data into a single working view — built for the people who actually have to act on it: field supervisors, entomologists, and national malaria programme staff.

Live network map

Species & resistance tracking

Logs Anopheles species composition and bioassay results over time, and flags emerging insecticide resistance early.

Outbreak risk scoring

Combines trap trends, rainfall, and case data into a rolling risk score per district — a ranked list, not just a map.

Role-based access

Our story

Built to outlast a single product

VDAM KC — Vector Dynamics and Malaria Knowledge for Control — started with a dashboard — a way to make vector surveillance data usable for the people fighting malaria on the ground. The long-term goal is bigger: an independent firm that can stand on its own and work alongside the major organizations already active in this fight, especially across endemic regions in Africa.

Where we're starting

A focused surveillance and analytics dashboard for vector-borne disease control — built by a founder who has done the fieldwork himself, from insectary operations to CDC light-trap deployment, and now writes the code to make sense of that data.

Where we're headed

A standalone firm capable of partnering with national programmes, research institutions, and global health organizations — building tools that endemic-region teams can eventually own and run themselves.

What we hold to

Four things that don't change as we grow

Rigor

Every risk score traces back to real field data. No dashboard number without a method behind it.

Local partnership

We build with the teams who will use this daily, in the regions where it matters — not for them from a distance.

Open collaboration

We design to plug into the tools and reporting formats health systems already use, rather than asking them to switch.

Long-term commitment

Malaria elimination is a decades-long effort. We're building a company structured to still be here for it.
