# VDAM KC — Capability Statement

**Vector Dynamics & Malaria Knowledge for Control**

*Turning mosquito field data into malaria control decisions — built for the realities of endemic regions.*

---

## Who we are

VDAM KC is an independent vector-surveillance and analytics firm building tools that turn entomological field data into decisions for malaria control. We are designed for the conditions endemic regions actually face — patchy connectivity, multiple vector species, and rising insecticide resistance — and built so that the national programmes and field teams who use our tools can eventually own and run them themselves.

## Core capabilities

- **Field data capture** — Offline-first logging of trap counts, larval-habitat surveys, and insecticide-resistance bioassays from a phone or tablet, with automatic sync once a signal returns.
- **Species & resistance tracking** — *Anopheles* species composition and WHO-standard bioassay results tracked over time, with early flags for emerging insecticide resistance.
- **AI-assisted specimen screening** — Image- and morphological-key-based identification as a screening aid, with deliberate taxonomic guardrails — cryptic species complexes are never over-resolved beyond what the evidence supports.
- **Risk scoring & forecasting** — Trap trends, rainfall, and clinical case data combined into rolling district-level risk scores and hotspot maps that update as new data arrives.
- **Policy-ready reporting** — Exports structured around the formats national malaria programmes already use for WHO reporting cycles.

## What sets us apart

- **Works where connectivity doesn't** — offline-first by design, built for rural trap networks rather than office Wi-Fi.
- **Species- and resistance-aware** — tracks what's actually driving transmission, not just raw trap counts.
- **Honest by design** — a screening aid, not a validated diagnostic device; our methods never claim more precision than the underlying data supports.
- **Built to be handed over** — structured so local teams and ministries can run it independently, not as a vendor dependency.
